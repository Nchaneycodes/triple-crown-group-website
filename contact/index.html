We are a service-area business in the Greater Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky metro. We work on-site across Boone, Kenton, and Campbell counties in Kentucky and Hamilton County in Ohio, rather than out of a storefront.

Cincinnati, Covington, Newport, Florence, Fort Mitchell, Erlanger, Crestview Hills, Blue Ash, Mason, West Chester, and Liberty Township, plus the surrounding Greater Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky area.

Yes. Most engagements are with small and mid-sized companies whose teams are expected to use AI but have not been given a clear way to start.
