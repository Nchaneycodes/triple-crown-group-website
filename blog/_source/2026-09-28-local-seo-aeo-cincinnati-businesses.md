**Quick answer:** To get recommended by AI assistants as a local business, focus on third-party corroboration rather than your own website. Google Business Profile, directory listings, review platforms, chamber and association pages, and local press are what assistants actually retrieve for "best X in Cincinnati" queries. Your business information must be byte-identical everywhere it appears, inconsistency creates entity confusion, and confused entities do not get named.

Someone in Mason opens ChatGPT and types "best AI consultant near Cincinnati." They get three names and a paragraph. There is no map pack, no page two, and no second chance.

Here is how local businesses become one of the three.

## Why local AEO works differently than local SEO

Traditional local SEO is heavily weighted toward **proximity** (how close you are to the searcher) plus relevance and prominence. That is why the map pack changes as you drive across town.

AI assistants mostly do not have your location, and when they do, they weight it less. What they have instead is text. So the question shifts from "who is closest" to "who does the web consistently describe as a Cincinnati provider of this thing."

That changes the priority order:

| Signal | Local SEO weight | Local AEO weight |
|---|---|---|
| Proximity to searcher | Very high | Low |
| Google Business Profile completeness | High | High |
| Citation consistency (NAP) | Moderate | Very high |
| Review volume and content | High | High |
| Third-party mentions and roundups | Moderate | Very high |
| On-site local content | Moderate | Moderate |
| Backlinks | High | Moderate |

The two rows that jump are citation consistency and third-party mentions. Those are where local AEO effort should concentrate.

## The foundation: entity consistency

An AI assistant assembling an answer about your business is reconciling information from a dozen sources. If those sources disagree, different phone numbers, "Inc." on one and "LLC" on another, two versions of your address. The assistant has lower confidence in the entity and is less likely to name it.

**What to make identical everywhere:**

- Legal business name, exactly as registered
- Address format, character for character, including suite designation style
- Phone number format
- Website URL, including whether it has a trailing slash and www
- Business category
- Description, ideally the same 1 to 2 sentence description everywhere

**Where to fix it:**

1. Google Business Profile
2. Bing Places
3. Apple Business Connect (feeds Apple Maps and Siri)
4. Your own website footer and contact page
5. LinkedIn company page
6. Facebook business page
7. Your Ohio or Kentucky Secretary of State registration
8. Chamber of commerce listings, Cincinnati USA Regional Chamber, NKY Chamber
9. Industry association directories
10. Review platforms
11. Any aggregator that has picked you up

That is an afternoon of work and it is the highest-leverage afternoon available to a local business on this topic.

## Google Business Profile, done properly

GBP is the most widely syndicated source of local business data on the web, which makes it disproportionately influential across every assistant, not just Google's.

**Complete every field.** Categories (primary and secondary), services with descriptions, service areas, hours, attributes, and the business description. Empty fields are lost signal.

**Post regularly.** GBP posts are indexed content. A short weekly post with real substance beats nothing, and most local businesses post nothing.

**Answer the Q&A section yourself.** You can ask and answer questions on your own profile. This is a direct, underused mechanism for putting question-and-answer text into a heavily retrieved source. Add the eight questions your customers actually ask.

**A note on address for service-area businesses:** if you do not have commercial space customers visit, use the service-area business setting, which hides the street address publicly while still verifying the business. Do not use a virtual mailbox or coworking address as a displayed address. It violates Google's guidelines and risks suspension. This is a common and expensive mistake.

## Reviews as AI training data

Reviews matter for AEO in a way they do not for traditional SEO: assistants read the *text*, not just the star rating.

A review saying "they helped our sales team actually use AI instead of just recommending tools" is a semantic signal that connects your business to a specific capability. Fifty five-star reviews with no text are worth less than fifteen detailed ones.

**Practical implications:**

- Ask for reviews that describe the specific problem solved, not just satisfaction. "If you're willing to leave a review, mentioning what we actually helped with is more useful than anything else."
- Spread reviews across platforms. Google, industry-specific platforms, and LinkedIn recommendations all get retrieved.
- Respond to reviews with substance. Your responses are text on the page too.
- Never fabricate reviews. Beyond being unethical and illegal, review platform detection has gotten good, and removal damages the signal you were trying to build.

## Third-party mentions: the highest-leverage work

For local AEO, being named on a page someone else controls is worth more than anything on your own site.

**Local directories and lists.** Any "best [category] in Cincinnati" list. These pages are structurally exactly what the query asks for, so they are retrieved constantly. Getting included is often as simple as asking.

**Chamber and association memberships.** Cincinnati USA Regional Chamber, Northern Kentucky Chamber, industry associations. Member directories are crawled and carry regional authority.

**Local business press.** Cincinnati Business Courier, LINK nky, Cincy Magazine, Soapbox. A single article mentioning your company can influence AI answers well out of proportion to its traffic.

**Podcasts and events with published text.** Transcripts and event pages are retrievable text. A local business podcast appearance with a published transcript is a durable citation asset.

**Partner and vendor pages.** If you are a certified partner of anything, make sure their directory listing is accurate and complete.

## On-site work that still matters

Your site will not carry the recommendation, but it needs to confirm the entity:

- **LocalBusiness schema** with your NAP, service area, and hours
- **Location mentioned naturally** in your title tags, homepage copy, and about page, "Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky," not stuffed
- **Service-area pages** that are substantively different from each other, not templated with the city name swapped. Thin templated city pages are recognized as such and can hurt
- **An FAQ section** answering the questions buyers actually ask, in question-and-answer form
- **AI crawlers allowed in robots.txt**, GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, OAI-SearchBot, Applebot-Extended, Google-Extended

## How to measure it

Build a tracking sheet of 20 to 40 local prompts:

- "Best [your category] in Cincinnati"
- "[Your category] near Northern Kentucky"
- "Who should I hire for [problem] in Cincinnati"
- "[Your category] in Covington / Florence / Blue Ash / Mason"
- "Compare [you] and [competitor]"

Run them monthly across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. Log whether you appear, in what position, whether the description is accurate, and what sources were cited.

The cited sources column is the most actionable thing you will produce. Those pages are your placement target list.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do local businesses show up in ChatGPT recommendations?**
Through third-party corroboration (Google Business Profile, directories, review text, local press, association listings) far more than through their own website.

**Does Google Business Profile affect AI results?**
Yes. It feeds Google's AI Overviews directly and is the most widely syndicated source of local business data, which makes it influential across other assistants too.

**How long does local AEO take?**
Entity consistency fixes can influence answers within weeks. Third-party mention building takes three to six months and is the more durable half.

**Can I pay to be included in AI recommendations?**
Not reliably. Be skeptical of anyone selling guaranteed placement. What you can pay for is legitimate directory inclusion, sponsorships that produce real pages, and PR that generates genuine coverage.

**What if AI describes my business incorrectly?**
Trace it. Usually one incorrect or outdated third-party source is propagating. Correct it there, and expect several weeks for the correction to work through.
