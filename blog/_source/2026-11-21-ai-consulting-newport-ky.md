**Quick answer:** For a customer-facing small business in Newport, the four things worth doing are inquiry response drafting, review response, local marketing content, and fixing your business listing data so AI assistants recommend you. That last one is the most overlooked and often the highest return. Under ten employees, this is a few hundred dollars and a couple of afternoons, not a consulting engagement.

Newport's business base runs heavily toward customer-facing operations, the Levee and riverfront hospitality corridor, Monmouth Street retail and restaurants, and the local service businesses serving Campbell County. Different problems than a manufacturer in Florence.

## The four things worth doing

### 1. Inquiry and booking response

**The problem:** inquiries arrive at all hours through email, web forms, social messages, and booking platforms. Response speed strongly affects conversion, and small businesses are slow not from indifference but because the owner is working.

**What AI does:** drafts a specific response referencing the actual question, ready to review and send from a phone in under a minute.

**What it's worth:** the single highest-return use for most small customer-facing businesses. No technical setup beyond a subscription and a documented prompt.

**The boundary:** review before sending. Full automation of customer communication at a small business is a bad trade, your responsiveness and personality are part of what people are buying.

### 2. Review response

**The problem:** responding to reviews matters for local visibility and for how prospective customers perceive you, particularly on negative reviews. It is also emotionally taxing and gets avoided.

**What AI does:** drafts responses that are professional, specific to what the reviewer said, and free of defensiveness. That last part is the real value, the hardest part of responding to an unfair review is not writing it, it is not writing what you first want to write.

**What it's worth:** consistent review response improves conversion from your listings and feeds the review text that AI assistants read when recommending local businesses.

**The boundary:** never fabricate, never argue, never disclose customer details in a public response.

### 3. Local marketing content

**The problem:** social posts, event promotion, email newsletters, and seasonal campaigns. Constant, small, and always the thing that does not get done.

**What AI does:** produces drafts and variants from a short description of what you want to communicate.

**The constraint:** your voice is a competitive asset at a local business. Generic content is worse than no content. Write down what your business sounds like (three sentences and a list of words you would never use) and put it in every prompt.

### 4. Getting found by AI assistants

This is the one most small businesses have not thought about and it may matter most.

**The change:** people increasingly ask ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity for local recommendations instead of searching. Those systems build recommendations primarily from third-party sources (Google Business Profile, directories, review platforms, local press) not from your website.

**What to do, in an afternoon:**

1. **Make your business information identical everywhere.** Exact same name, address format, phone, and description on Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places, Yelp, Facebook, and any directory you appear in. Inconsistency creates entity confusion and confused entities do not get recommended.
2. **Complete every field on your Google Business Profile.** Categories, services, hours, attributes, description. Empty fields are lost signal.
3. **Use the Q&A section on your own profile.** You can ask and answer questions there. Add the eight questions customers actually ask. This puts question-and-answer text into a heavily retrieved source.
4. **Ask for reviews that describe specifics.** AI assistants read review text, not just star counts. A review saying what you actually did is worth more than five saying "great!"
5. **Get listed in local roundups.** Any "best in Newport" or "best in Northern Kentucky" list. These pages are exactly what the query asks for, so they get retrieved constantly.

**Cost:** an afternoon. **Value:** potentially being one of three businesses named when someone asks an assistant for a recommendation.
