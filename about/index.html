About

Noah Chaney, founder.

My focus has never been the technical jargon behind AI. It's how a sales rep or a marketing team actually uses it, day to day, and what genuinely works for them, not what looks good in a demo.

I got here by working my way through a range of industries over 8 years in software, the last 5 of those focused on AI solutions. I started selling tech solutions to construction firms across Greater Cincinnati, then moved into dealerships, then shifted into AI for small and mid sized business owners. Today I sell AI solutions to some of the largest businesses across North America. That path is what led me back to serving local business owners directly, helping them deploy AI in their sales and marketing teams right here in Greater Cincinnati.

That's what Triple Crown Group represents. Winning on every front, the grand slam, the home run, the triple crown, making sure a system is optimized from every angle, not just one. It's also a nod to our roots here in Kentucky.
