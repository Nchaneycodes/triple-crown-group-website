How We Work

Three Ways We Work With You

Here's exactly what each stage includes.

TIER 01

The Discovery Audit

We find out where your time and money are actually going.

Two weeks. We look at your busiest, most repetitive workflow, the one costing you the most right now. At the end, you get a full report in plain language: the biggest inefficiencies we found, where AI could help, and if it makes sense, exactly what an ongoing partnership would look like for your business, down to the hours and the cost.

Fixed price: $1,500. If we do not find at least one fix worth more than what you paid, the audit is free. No obligation to go further.

TIER 02

Train and Build

We come in and build it with your team.

We train your team on the tools and workflows that came out of the audit, and build the specific automations and AI agents your business actually needs. Not off the shelf software, built around how you work. You end this stage with real tools in place and a team that knows how to use them.

Scope and cost are custom to what the audit found.

TIER 03

The Retainer

We become your AI department, without the hire.

Hiring someone full time to own this costs tens of thousands of dollars a year before you even count training and turnover risk. Pulling one of your best people off their real job to keep up with it costs you in a different way. Bring us on instead.

We stay on as your outsourced AI function: deploying new agents as you need them, holding regular office hours, troubleshooting when something breaks, and keeping things current as your business and the tools change. Scoped in hours each month, priced to what your business actually needs.

Common questions

Questions we get asked.

Which tier is right for me?

Start with the audit. It tells us, and you, exactly what the next step should be.

Can I just do the audit and stop there?

Yes. Plenty of people do. You keep the report either way.

What does staying on actually include?

Office hours, troubleshooting, new agents as you need them, and ongoing recommendations as things change.

Do you work outside Greater Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky?

Yes. The work translates well remotely.

How much does the retainer cost?

It depends on your business and what the audit finds. We will show you the exact hours and cost before you commit to anything.

What if I already have someone doing some of this?

Then we work alongside them, or hand them better tools to do it with. Either way, you are not paying for a full time hire you do not need yet.
