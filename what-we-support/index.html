Common questions

Questions we get asked.

What does an AI audit actually involve?

We look at how your sales and marketing teams work today, where the time goes, and which tasks AI could realistically take on. You get a clear picture of where you are now and a short list of where AI would help, ordered by what is worth doing first.

Do we have to replace the tools we already use?

Usually not. Most teams already pay for tools that do more than they realize. We audit what you have before recommending anything new, and sometimes the honest answer is to use what you already own properly.

What is a custom AI agent?

Something built around one of your workflows rather than a general purpose assistant. For a sales rep that might prepare call notes and follow ups. For leadership it might assemble a weekly roll up. It runs on your systems and follows your process.

How long does this take?

An audit is usually a few weeks. Training and rollout depend on how big your team is and how many workflows you want covered. We scope it with you before you commit to anything.
